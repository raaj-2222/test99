06 / SCIENTIFIC INTEGRITY

What these findings mean

01

What is the trend?

Sen's slope estimates the median rate of change across year pairs. A 95% slope interval describes uncertainty under the estimator's assumptions. Rainfall values are accumulated to millimetres per period.

02

Is it significant?

A two-sided Mann–Kendall test yields a p-value. Benjamini–Hochberg correction then controls the false discovery rate across five cities for each variable and period; we use q < 0.05.

03

What are the limits?

These are five NASA POWER grid-based point samples, not an India-wide pixel map. Tests assume independent annual values and don't adjust for autocorrelation or abrupt dataset changes. Trends do not establish causes.
